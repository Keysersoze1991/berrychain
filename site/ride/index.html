About the game

MTB Crazy is a side-on stunt racer in the spirit of the old Game Boy motocross games, swapped to a mountain bike. Twenty courses in four tiers, Shore, Reef, Open Sea and Storm, each tier with bigger jumps, more loops and nastier drops than the last. Flips and big air count as tricks, and every backflip or frontflip you land takes a second off your time. The clock runs from the start gate to the finish flag; crashing puts you back at the last checkpoint with the clock still running, so a clean run is a fast run.

0.2 BERRY for a clean run. Every run is recorded, pedal stroke by pedal stroke. When the race purse opens, finishing a course without a crash will earn 0.2 BERRY on BerryChain, paid as a letter to your chest with the coins attached, once per course per registered name. BERRY has no price and no value; it is the coin you write letters with. Until the purse opens, your best times live on this device only.
